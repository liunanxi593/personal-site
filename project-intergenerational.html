Intergenerational Communication

Background

This project explores the emotional distance between generations. During my undergraduate studies, I began to look at how technology can serve as a bridge rather than a barrier, using light as a medium to visualize conversation depth.

Problem

Young and older family members often find it difficult to initiate deep conversations. Emotional defenses and a lack of real-time feedback on the "mood" of the room can lead to shallow, repetitive interactions.

Approach

The system uses Affective Computing and Chinese BERT models to analyze dialogue in real-time. The core of the interaction is a smart lamp that shifts its color temperature based on the emotional resonance of the speakers.
